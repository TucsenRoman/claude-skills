Building blocks

Every sheet uses the same definitions. One meaning per word.

Window

The last 90 days up to today. Copy says "90 days", never "13 weeks".

Full weeks

Monday–Sunday weeks fully inside the window, before this week. Only for week facts: lowest, best, "done it before", the bars.

1"Fits with your saving goal" and bills

Today: limit + saving goal ≤ income, bills ignored, so it can say "fits" when rent doesn't. ", bills included" read as if bills were inside the limit.

AFits with your $200 saving goal and $280 of bills
BLeaves $62 a week after bills and saving

2What counts as a save

Real data read $465 a week against about $350 really saved: moves between two savings accounts at the same bank count, and outside deposits (checks) count.

Proposed defaultMoney moved from your own account into savings or investments. Same-bank savings-to-savings and unmatched deposits don't count.
PlusYou choose what counts, per account and per transaction (in design).

3One usual week everywhere

The calendar's week totals and the goal check must leave bills out the same way the sheets do. Confirm before trusting the bars.

4Refunds

Does a refund lower the week it lands in, the week of the purchase, or neither (today)?

5"+1 day/month" assumes the money stays

If Saving moves it to an account Cushion doesn't count, the days don't grow. Say so, or drop the row then.

6Hidden accounts and Cushion

A hidden account's spending still counts in Cushion's daily spending, but not on the Spending sheet.
